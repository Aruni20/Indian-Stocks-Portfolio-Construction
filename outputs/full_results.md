## Method notes

Minimum variance and maximum Sharpe portfolios use 200,000 seeded long-only Monte-Carlo portfolios. The eigen portfolio standardizes daily returns, computes PCA by SVD, and selects the best-Sharpe portfolio among the first five absolute-loading components. Annualization uses 250 trading days and the Sharpe risk-free rate is 1%.
